## The problem
Students practise from PDF question banks, but real exams are taken on a computer, with a timer, a question palette and no answer key in sight. Practising on paper doesn't prepare you for that.

## The solution
PDF Examify converts your PDF question bank into an interactive CBT that simulates the real exam interface, then shows a full result analysis at the end.

## How it works
1. **Extract:** copy the provided prompt and upload your PDF to DeepSeek through the built-in chat panel. It converts your MCQs, including passages and underlined words, into structured JSON.
2. **Import:** paste the output and click **Import Questions & Continue**.
3. **Configure:** set up your test.
4. **Take the test:** a timed, exam-style interface that submits automatically when time runs out.
5. **Review:** see your score instantly, then go through every question with the correct answers.

## Features
- **PDF to test** conversion
- **Timed tests** with auto-submit, for real exam conditions
- **Instant results** with performance analysis
- **Question review** with correct answers after submission
- **Default question set** to try it out without a PDF

> **Limitations:** questions based on diagrams or images can't be extracted yet, and your PDF must include the answer key for results to be checked.
